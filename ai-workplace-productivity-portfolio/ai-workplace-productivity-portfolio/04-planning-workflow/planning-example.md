# Planning Workflow — Getting the TaskFlow Launch Back on Track

## Goal
Get the TaskFlow product launch back on a confirmed, achievable track within 24 hours, resolving the blocking decisions before the Friday launch deadline.

## Mechanisms
How we'll achieve the goal:
- Resolve the technical blocker (onboarding bug) first, since every other decision depends on it.
- Sequence the remaining decisions (launch date → paid ads → discount strategy) in order of urgency.
- Communicate each confirmed decision immediately to the teams who depend on it (marketing, ads, design).

## Phases
1. **Diagnose** — confirm the real fix timeline for the critical bug.
2. **Decide** — lock the launch date (Friday or delayed) based on the diagnosis.
3. **Execute** — book ads and finalize the discount strategy in line with the confirmed date.
4. **Communicate** — send a single unified update to all stakeholders.

## Tasks
| Phase | Task | Owner | Deadline |
|---|---|---|---|
| Diagnose | Meet engineering lead to confirm bug fix + testing timeline | Me | Today, AM |
| Decide | Confirm Friday launch or 2–3 day delay based on diagnosis | Me + Eng Lead | Today, by EOD |
| Decide | Define minimum acceptable bug-fix scope for launch | Me | Today, by EOD |
| Execute | Book or pause paid ads based on confirmed date | Marketing | Today (ads deadline is tomorrow) |
| Execute | Finalize launch discount strategy | Me + Marketing | Tomorrow, AM |
| Communicate | Send unified update to Engineering, Marketing, Design, CEO | Me | Today, after decisions are locked |

---
**Why this order:** The bug-fix timeline is the single fact that every other decision (launch date, ad booking, discount messaging) depends on, so it had to be resolved first rather than planned around in parallel guesses.
