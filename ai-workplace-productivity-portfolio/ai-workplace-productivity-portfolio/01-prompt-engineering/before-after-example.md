# Before / After: Prompt Improvement Example

## Before (Weak Prompt)
> "Write an email about the deadline change."

## Initial Result
A generic, vague email. It didn't specify who the audience was, what the new date was, why the deadline changed, or what tone to use — so the AI guessed on all four, producing a message that was too casual for a client and missing a concrete next date.

## After (Improved Prompt — C.A.R.E.)
> **Context:** Our 6-person team is midway through a client project. The final deliverable is due soon, but we need to shift the deadline by two weeks due to a scope change requested mid-project.
> **Action:** Write an email to the client explaining the deadline change and the new date. Ask them to confirm the new date works on their end.
> **Role:** Act as a calm, clear, and experienced project coordinator. Communicate with empathy and professionalism.
> **Expected Output:** 150 words, 3 paragraphs, professional tone, directed to a client. Include a clear next step and a deadline for their reply.

## Improved Result
A concise, professional email: paragraph 1 states the change plainly, paragraph 2 gives the one-line reason and the new date, paragraph 3 asks for confirmation by a specific day. No filler, no over-apologizing, and a clear call to action.

## What Improved
1. **Audience and role were explicit**, so the tone matched a client-facing message instead of an internal one.
2. **Context (why the change happened) was provided**, so the AI didn't invent a reason.
3. **Expected Output constraints (length, format, tone) removed guesswork** about structure.
4. **A concrete next step was requested**, so the email ended with an action instead of trailing off.
