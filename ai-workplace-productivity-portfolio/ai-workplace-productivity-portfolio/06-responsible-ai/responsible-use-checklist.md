# Responsible AI Use — Project Coordinator

## The Six Concepts Applied

### Accuracy
Every specific number or fact the AI produces in my drafts (like the "23% increase" example) gets checked against a real source before it's used — fluent output is never treated as automatically correct.

### Accountability
I sign off as the final "editor of record" on every AI-assisted message or plan in this portfolio — if something is wrong after I send it, that responsibility is mine, not the tool's.

### Confidentiality
No real client names, internal financial figures, or proprietary project details appear anywhere in this repository — all examples (e.g., "TaskFlow," "[Client]") are fictional or anonymized, since this repo is public.

### Privacy
I never paste real personal data (employee names, personal emails, phone numbers) into AI prompts or into this portfolio — all names used in examples are placeholders.

### Bias
When asking AI to draft messages to different audiences (Section 2, tone modulation), I checked that the AI wasn't defaulting to stereotyped assumptions about a given audience's expertise or interest level, and adjusted where needed.

### Human Oversight
Every AI output in this portfolio — the email drafts, the status brief, the plan — passed through a human review step before being marked final; none were used as-is straight from the AI.

## Data Sensitivity Classification
Applied to information used elsewhere in this portfolio:

| Information Item | Classification | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| Launch date and general rollout status (Section 3) | 🟢 Green | Internal-but-shareable project status, no sensitive detail. |
| Possible contractor budget overrun, unconfirmed (Section 3) | 🟡 Amber | Financial and unconfirmed — needs internal confirmation before wider sharing. |
| Real client name, contract terms, or specific pricing figures | 🔴 Red | Confidential client/commercial data — never placed in this public repository; always replaced with a placeholder like [Client]. |
